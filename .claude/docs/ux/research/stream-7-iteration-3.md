## B. A discreet scrollbar that shows what is left (UXF-11)

| WAN | Source | What it says | Used for |
|---|---|---|---|
| 120 | [Apple HIG: Scroll views](https://developer.apple.com/design/human-interface-guidelines/scroll-views) | The indicator "typically appears after people begin scrolling"; on macOS it "shows whether the currently visible content is near the beginning, middle, or end"; "Because scroll indicators aren't always visible, it can be helpful to make it obvious when content extends beyond the view" | Apple itself asks for an extra cue when the bar hides |
| 121 | [Apple: Appearance settings](https://support.apple.com/guide/mac-help/change-appearance-settings-mchlp1225/mac) | Show scroll bars: Automatically, When scrolling, Always | The rail must behave the same under all three |
| 122 | [NN/g: Scrolling and scrollbars](https://www.nngroup.com/articles/scrolling-and-scrollbars/) | "Offer a scrollbar if an area has scrolling content"; "Hide scrollbars if all content is visible"; "Unless your scrollbars look like scrollbars, users might not notice them" | Always present when the document overflows; hidden when it fits; shaped like a scrollbar thumb |
| 123 | [NN/g: Alternatives to pagination](https://www.nngroup.com/articles/alternatives-pagination-listing-pages/) | A persistent count whose border "increased to show the user's location" | Persistent position cue (listing pages, not reading) |
| 124 | [WebKit: Styling scrollbars](https://webkit.org/blog/363/styling-scrollbars/) | With `::-webkit-scrollbar` present "WebKit will turn off its built-in scrollbar rendering" | Styling the real bar removes the overlay bar; the hover rule in tv-app.css shrinks the column by 5 px |
| 125 | [MDN: ::-webkit-scrollbar](https://developer.mozilla.org/en-US/docs/Web/CSS/Reference/Selectors/::-webkit-scrollbar) | Non-standard; a non-`auto` `scrollbar-color`/`scrollbar-width` overrides it | Do not mix the two on one element |
| 126 | [MDN: scrollbar-gutter](https://developer.mozilla.org/en-US/docs/Web/CSS/Reference/Properties/scrollbar-gutter) | Overlay scrollbars take no gutter; classic ones do | An overlay element costs no layout width |
| 127 | [Microsoft Learn: Scroll viewer controls](https://learn.microsoft.com/en-us/windows/apps/design/controls/scroll-controls) | Panning indicator: a "2 pixel wide vertical line at the right edge"; under the pointer "it morphs into the traditional scrollbar" | Thin at rest, wider and draggable on hover |
| 128 | [GTK 4: overlay-scrolling](https://docs.gtk.org/gtk4/property.ScrolledWindow.overlay-scrolling.html) | Overlaid "as narrow indicators" unless a mouse is present | Same pattern on GNOME |
| 129 | [VS Code: Basic editing](https://code.visualstudio.com/docs/editing/codebasics) | Find results show "in the editor, overview ruler, and minimap" | Marks on the track: noted, out of scope |
| 130 | [Amazon: Kindle for Web](https://www.amazon.com/gp/help/customer/display.html?nodeId=TT200NNkr2BE4Jnsy9), [WebKit: scroll-driven animations](https://webkit.org/blog/17101/a-guide-to-scroll-driven-animations-with-just-css/) | Kindle offers "time left in chapter"; WebKit shows a CSS-only progress bar, "available in Safari 26 beta" | Time-left readout on demand; CSS-only not yet safe on older macOS WebKit |
