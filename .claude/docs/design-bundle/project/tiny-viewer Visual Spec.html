Visual Design Specification

The token system, type scale, spacing, component states, and accessibility rules behind tiny-viewer's five screens. Every decision traces to a Usage Research Datum (URD) or Design Requirement (DR) from the research foundation.

Focus ring

Keyboard focus shows a 2px accent ring with a 1.5px surface gap — visible on every tab, row, button, and the command-palette input. Never removed; the app is keyboard-first.

schema.md

Reduced motion

URD-10 · DR-10

Under prefers-reduced-motion the ⚡ pulse, blip ring, and shimmer collapse to a static state: the live dot holds full opacity, the streaming caret stops blinking. No information lives in motion alone.

Live watch ≠ scrolljack

URD-10 · DR-10

Streaming appends at the bottom; the user's scroll position is never moved unless Pin scroll is explicitly toggled on. Status is conveyed by text, not just colour.

Hit targets

Tabs, tree rows, palette results, and buttons all meet a ≥28px interactive height with generous padding; the close × expands its target on hover rather than shrinking the tab.
